## Brand & Style

The brand represents the next generation of creative video editing—where generative AI meets high-end cinematic craft. It serves storytellers, filmmakers, and digital creators who demand the precision of professional studio suites paired with the instantaneous clarity of modern text-driven editing.

The visual mood blends modern editorial restraint with vibrant chromatic precision:
- **Tone:** Artfully crafted, hyper-focused, effortless, and premium.
- **Design Style:** Modern Editorial Minimalist. It pairs expansive white canvases, razor-sharp hairline borders, and sophisticated serif typography with saturated, high-octane accents drawn from film stock calibration cards and color grading primaries (vibrant orange-red, royal electric blue, neon lime, and warm amber).
- **Interface Experience:** Visual noise is stripped down so video frames and multi-track timelines command full attention. Micro-copy and status indicators lean into clean, monospaced or structured sans-serif utilities, balancing human storytelling with machine precision.

## Colors

The palette uses a luminous, clinical white base accented by pure spectrum primaries that reflect professional video grading and filmstrip calibration ribbons.

- **Primary (`#E03616`):** Director Crimson-Orange. Used for primary CTAs, active live-record states, playhead scrubbers, and destructive or cut indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** Royal Cobalt Blue. Anchors timeline video tracks, selection bounding boxes, aspect-ratio controls, and focused inspector panes.
- **Tertiary (`#84CC16`):** Electric Lime / Audio Green. Reserved for clean voiceover wave forms, automated b-roll triggers, dynamic badges, and successful render/sync indicators.
- **Amber Accent (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber for subtitle tracks, transcript highlights, and system warnings.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:**
  - Background Base: `#FFFFFF` (pure white canvas)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F8F9FA` (canvas secondary, timeline gutters, and pill toggles)
  - Surface Raised / Nested: `#F1F3F5` (nested transcript boxes, clip track backgrounds)
  - Border Hairline: `#E5E7EB` (neutral separation rules, card outlines, toolbar dividers)
  - Text Primary: `#111827` (deep graphite black)
  - Text Secondary: `#4B5563` and `#6B7280` (metadata, durations, and secondary labels)

## Typography

The typography pairs traditional literary elegance with crisp modern software engineering:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Newsreader**, an authoritative, high-contrast serif. Use italic weights deliberately on focal keywords (e.g., "*Your cuts.*") to inject kinetic editorial flair into cinematic claims.
- **Body & Interface Elements:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, accessible numerals, and friendly legibility at dense editor scales.
- **Technical Readouts & Micro-Labels:** Set in **Space Grotesk** in uppercase or semi-condensed monospace formats (`label-code`, `label-timecode`). This anchors frame rates (`23.976 fps`), timecodes (`00:01:24:18`), audio decibel readings, and feature tags (`STUDIO ENGINE`, `BI-SYNC`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model divides clearly into two interconnected domains:
1. **Marketing & Showcase Pages:** A structured 12-column fluid grid framed with generous whitespace (`margin: 3rem`, scaling down to `1.25rem` on mobile screens). Ample vertical padding (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolates product cards and interactive media tiers, giving each module an editorial presence.
2. **Studio Editor Canvas:** A fixed-height, fluid-width workspace divided into modular panels:
   - Left: Script & Transcript dock (fixed 320px–380px).
   - Center: Dynamic aspect viewport (16:9, 9:16, 1:1) over a horizontal multi-track sequence.
   - Right: Inspector & Color toolsets (fixed 280px–320px).
   - Bottom: Multi-tier timeline with 36px fixed track heights and 2px hairline inter-track gaps.

Responsive transitions collapse left and right utility sidebars into floating slide-over sheets below 1024px viewport widths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat crisp surfaces layered atop subtle surface tone shifts rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** `#FFFFFF` main viewports and marketing canvas.
- **Container Tiers (Level 1):** `#F8F9FA` card bodies, docked studio panels, and timeline headers outlined with a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`).
- **Nested Controls (Level 2):** `#F1F3F5` for input fields, text transcripts, and unselected pill chips.
- **Ambient Focus / Action Shadows:** Interactive hero buttons and floating dialogs utilize a hyper-diffused, tinted shadow:
  - Crimson CTA Glow: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(224, 54, 22, 0.3)`
  - Elevated Sheet Drop: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`
- **Glass Overlays:** Modals, floating playhead controls, and top navigation bars use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface opacity and a delicate bottom border (`#E5E7EB`).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded corner geometry:
- **Cards & Showcase Modules:** `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem radius) creating a friendly, high-end consumer-grade software feel.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Rounded full pills (`9999px`) for action chips, timeline filter tags, and primary CTAs.
- **Studio Panels & Modals:** `rounded-xl` (12px to 14px radius) for docked tools and internal containers.
- **Track Clips & Small Badges:** Compact `rounded-md` (6px radius) to maintain structural density on narrow timeline lanes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Director):** Full-bleed vibrant crimson-orange (`#E03616`), pill-shaped, crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), bold font weight (`600`), styled with a subtle ambient glow on hover (`rgba(224, 54, 22, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, dark graphite text (`#111827`), hover background `#F8F9FA`.
- **Media Play Button:** Circular pill containing a subtle red or neutral icon, paired with an inline label.

### Interactive Pills & Chips
- **Category & Status Badges:** Soft pastel tints paired with vibrant saturated text:
  - Script Cut Tag: `#FEE2E2` background with `#E03616` text.
  - Smart Aspect Tag: `#DBEAFE` background with `#2563EB` text.
  - Active Voice Node: `#ECFCCB` background with `#4D7C0F` text.
- **LUT & Filter Selectors:** Compact squircle chips (`rounded-md`, 40x40px) displaying technical film stock names (`KODAK`, `FUJI`, `ARRI`) in high-contrast miniature typography.

### Input Fields & Transcript Blocks
- **Transcript Textbox:** Nested `#F8F9FA` card with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` stroke. Strikethrough or excised audio segments highlighted in crimson tint (`#FEE2E2`) with red crossed-out text (`#DC2626`).
- **Numeric & Scrubber Inputs:** Clean inline numeric values with trailing unit tags (`114.0%`, `+18 px`, `23.976 fps`) in monospaced space lettering.

### Studio Multi-Track Timeline
- **Track Containers:** Horizontal bands (`36px` to `48px` tall) surfaced in `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA`.
- **Master Video Clips:** Saturated royal blue blocks (`#2563EB`) with crisp white micro-labels, audio scrub lines, and miniature right-aligned metadata tags (`4K 24p`).
- **Voice / Audio Clips:** Acid lime-green blocks (`#84CC16`) with a darker contrast waveform (`#3F6212`) indicating spoken cadences.
- **Playhead Needle:** Vertical `2px` crimson line (`#E03616`) topped by a diamond scrub head and floating timecode badge.

### Cards & Feature Panels
- **Standard Card:** Minimum 16px corner radii, `#FFFFFF` fill, bordered with delicate `1px solid #E5E7EB`. Internal content flows from monospaced metadata badges down to serif titles and nested visual mockup panes.
